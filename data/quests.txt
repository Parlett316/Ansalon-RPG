# data/quests.txt -- hand-authored quest definitions, one QUEST/END block
# each. Bound to a quest-giver POI by a QUEST <char> <quest-id> line in a
# zone file (see docs/ZONE_NOTES.md) -- that POI must already have a TALK
# line, since a quest is offered/updated/turned in through talking to it.
# Grammar and design model: docs/QUEST_NOTES.md.
#
# Milestone 51 shipped one quest as a proof of concept for the engine --
# offer/accept/progress/turn-in, the journal, and a Slay objective (the one
# kind that needed new persistent state, see docs/QUEST_NOTES.md). Milestone
# 52 (below) is the real content pass: four more quests from ordinary NPCs,
# deliberately covering VISIT and TALK objectives and a REQUIRE condition
# other than knight for the first time -- see docs/QUEST_NOTES.md's
# "Shipped quests" section. Milestone 53 (below) is Knight of the Sword
# advancement -- REQUIRE sword_eligible, this project's first quest to mix
# two objective kinds, and REWARD_KNIGHT_SWORD. The Dragonlance magical
# items milestone (below) adds solamnic_armor -- REQUIRE sword_knight (a
# Sword Knight specifically, distinct from sword_eligible above) and
# REWARD_SOLAMNIC_ARMOR, this project's first item-granting quest reward.
# The Order of the Rose milestone (below) adds measure_of_roses -- REQUIRE
# rose_eligible (Solamnia's highest rank, same compound-condition shape as
# sword_eligible) and REWARD_KNIGHT_ROSE.
# DELIVER (below, ore_for_the_forge) is this project's first carried-item
# objective: an actual character::ItemKind::QuestItem, granted by a zone
# POI's GRANTS_ITEM line and consumed on turn-in, rather than a query over
# state the game already tracked the way VISIT/TALK/SLAY are.
# staff_of_striking_curing (below) is this project's second item-granting
# reward after solamnic_armor -- REQUIRE cleric and REWARD_STAFF_OF_
# STRIKING_CURING, granting character::kStaffOfStrikingCuringName (see
# docs/CHARACTER_NOTES.md's "Magic items").
# frostreaver_salvage (below) is this project's third item-granting reward
# -- REQUIRE str_13 and REWARD_FROSTREAVER, granting
# character::kFrostreaverName (see docs/CHARACTER_NOTES.md's "Magic
# items"). First quest to gate on a raw ability score rather than
# race/class/knight-rank.

QUEST road_wolves
NAME The Wolves on the Solace Road
OFFER "Three wolves, bold as brass, worrying travelers on the south road," the notice reads, in a hand you don't recognize. "Whoever thins their numbers has the thanks of Solace -- and its steel."
ACCEPT You memorize the particulars: the south road, and wolves bold enough to trouble it in daylight now.
PROGRESS The notice is still pinned where you left it. The wolves, presumably, are still out there.
COMPLETE Someone's pinned a fresh scrap beneath where the notice used to be: "Paid, with my thanks -- a grateful farmer." Tucked behind it, sure enough, is a small pouch of steel.
GIVER the Notice Board
SLAY wolf 3 Kill three timber wolves on the roads around Solace
REWARD_STEEL 40
REWARD_XP 90
END

QUEST inn_supply_run
NAME The Larder's Watch
OFFER Otik nods toward the kitchen doorway, expression gone serious for once. "The hunters who keep this kitchen stocked won't walk the north trails anymore -- say there's goblins thick enough to smell it from the fire road. I can't cook what nobody'll bring me."
ACCEPT You know the trails he means. If the goblins want them, they can have them once you're done arguing about it.
PROGRESS Otik glances toward the empty hooks over the kitchen door where the game usually hangs. "Trails still quiet, from what I hear. Not the good kind of quiet."
COMPLETE Otik actually stops wiping the bar when you tell him. "Well. Hunters'll be glad to hear it, and so am I -- can't fry what I don't have." He digs a pouch out from under the counter without being asked twice.
GIVER Otik, at the Inn of the Last Home
SLAY goblin 3 Clear the goblins off the trails north of Solace
REWARD_STEEL 30
REWARD_XP 70
END

QUEST word_for_the_tower
NAME Word for the Tower
OFFER The sentry doesn't lower his spear, but his voice drops out of its official register. "Vingaard's gone quiet and we don't know why. If you're headed toward Palanthas anyway -- and most people passing through here are -- tell them the Tower still holds. Somebody down there ought to know that, even if nobody's asked."
ACCEPT You commit the message to memory, plain as he gave it: the Tower still holds.
PROGRESS "Still holding," the sentry says, in case you'd forgotten what you're carrying. "Word out of Palanthas would be nice, if you hear any."
COMPLETE "Palanthas heard it, did they." The sentry's shoulders ease, fractionally -- the closest thing to relief his posture allows. "Good. That's one less thing I have to wonder about tonight."
GIVER the sentry at High Clerist's Tower
VISIT palanthas Carry word of the Tower's situation to Palanthas
REWARD_STEEL 30
REWARD_XP 60
END

QUEST bazaar_road_raiders
NAME Raiders on the Bazaar Road
OFFER The Watchman doesn't take his eyes off the market crowd while he talks. "Pickpockets I can live with -- part of the job. Hobgoblins hitting the caravan road into the bazaar, that's a different problem, and one truncheon won't solve it. You look like you might carry something sharper."
ACCEPT You take his meaning. Somebody's going to have to deal with the hobgoblins, and it isn't going to be the man with the truncheon.
PROGRESS The Watchman's eyes are still on the crowd, not on you. "Caravans still coming in light-handed and late. Whatever you're doing out there, it isn't done yet."
COMPLETE For the first time, the Watchman actually looks at you instead of the crowd. "Caravans came in on time and in full today. First time in weeks. I'll see you're paid for it, and gladly."
GIVER the City Watchman in Kalaman
SLAY hobgoblin 2 Drive the hobgoblins off the bazaar road
REWARD_STEEL 45
REWARD_XP 100
END

QUEST kin_beyond_the_border
NAME Kin Beyond the Border
OFFER The Warder studies you a moment longer than she studies most -- weighing something in your face that has nothing to do with whether you're a threat. "You are kin enough that I will ask instead of simply refusing you. Qualinesti sent no word before the dragonarmies came south, and none since. If you carry blood enough to be received there, carry this instead: word that Silvanesti still stands, sealed but standing."
ACCEPT You understand the weight of what she's asking, even stripped of its careful phrasing: reach Qualinesti, find one of her own kind to hear it, and say that Silvanesti endures.
PROGRESS The Warder inclines her head slightly, the barest acknowledgment. "The word is not yet carried. I have not asked you again, and I will not -- it is not the way of my people to press."
COMPLETE "Then it is said, and heard." Something in the Warder's bearing loosens, almost imperceptibly. "That is enough. It was always going to have to be enough."
GIVER the Silvanesti Warder
REQUIRE elf
TALK qualinesti:E Carry word to the Elven Sentinel in Qualinesti that Silvanesti still stands
REWARD_STEEL 30
REWARD_XP 60
END

QUEST named_in_fact
NAME In Fact as Well as Blood
OFFER "The Crown's insignia won't carry you the rest of the way, and you know it as well as I do." The Sword Knight's voice doesn't rise, but it doesn't invite argument either. "Prove yourself past it and I'll carry your name to the Council myself. Walk to the far plains and back on your own two feet -- thirty days there and back is no idle errand -- and somewhere on that road, meet a draconian blade to blade and come home standing. The tale of the rest, I'll ask for myself, when you're back."
ACCEPT You understand what he isn't spelling out: the road itself is the test, not just the fighting at the end of it. Whatever else it asks of you between here and the Plains of Dust, you'll know it when it comes.
PROGRESS The Sword Knight studies the horizon rather than you. "The road's still there. So is the duel, if you haven't found it yet. Neither one shortens for waiting."
COMPLETE The Sword Knight listens to the whole of it without interrupting -- the miles, the draconian's blade turned against you and answered, and, worked in almost as an afterthought, the rest: three strangers whose worries you took seriously enough to solve, a beggar's portion you didn't grudge, a debt of kindness you paid without being asked, something broken you set right before you thought to mention it. When you finish, he's quiet a moment. "That's a council hearing I don't expect to lose." He extends a hand, Crown-scarred like his own. "Then it's decided in fact, same as it was in blood. Welcome to the Sword."
GIVER the Sword Knight at High Clerist's Tower
REQUIRE sword_eligible
VISIT plains_of_dust Make the long road south to the Plains of Dust and back
SLAY baaz 1 Meet a draconian in single combat and come home standing
REWARD_STEEL 60
REWARD_XP 150
REWARD_KNIGHT_SWORD
END

QUEST solamnic_armor
NAME A Circle of Knights
OFFER The Knight of the Circle doesn't raise his voice, but doesn't need to. "Sword-rank is your own doing. What the Circle wants, before it does anything more for you, is a fellow Knight's word standing alongside it -- not just my own sense of you. If the Garrison Knight can speak to what he's seen, I'll carry it to the Circle myself."
ACCEPT You understand what's being asked -- not a new task exactly, just the Garrison Knight's word standing beside your own, wherever that word already stands.
PROGRESS The Knight of the Circle is still waiting on the Garrison Knight's account of you. Whether that's already given or still to come, he hasn't said.
COMPLETE The old knight listens to what the Garrison Knight has to say about you -- not much, in as few words as the man ever uses, but enough. "That's the kind of account the Circle trusts," he says, and for the first time something like approval crosses his face. He produces a set of plate, tooled with three orders' worth of markings, and a shield to match. "Wear it as it was meant. You've earned that much."
GIVER the Knight of the Circle at High Clerist's Tower
REQUIRE sword_knight
TALK high_clerist_tower:K Have the Garrison Knight vouch for you before the Circle
REWARD_STEEL 40
REWARD_XP 100
REWARD_SOLAMNIC_ARMOR
END

QUEST measure_of_roses
NAME The Measure of Roses
OFFER "The Sword's a title the Order hands out for courage. What the Rose asks for is patience -- and I don't just mean yours." Something about her formality is drawn tighter than the Sword Knight's own, like she's measuring not just the candidate but the whole shape of the request. "Walk the same road again, if you're willing. Thirty days out to the far plains and back, same as before -- and somewhere on it, put down something worse than a draconian. An ogre won't ask you to spare it. See that you don't need it to."
ACCEPT You understand this isn't the same test dressed up twice, whatever the road has in common with the last one. The Rose doesn't care how the fight ends so much as how you get there -- and how you leave everything else along the way.
PROGRESS The road's the same road. Whether you've walked all of it again, she couldn't say from here.
COMPLETE The Rose Knight listens the way the Sword Knight never quite managed -- unhurried, unreadable, weighing each part of it separately before she weighs the whole. The ogre first: point taken, blade turned rather than driven home, the same discipline the Order asks of every duel. Then the rest, in no order she seems to care about -- a question you answered honestly when a lie would have served you better, three times you gave more than you were asked, three times you stayed when leaving would have cost you nothing, and somewhere on the road home, something broken that you took the trouble to mend. When you finish, she's quiet long enough that you wonder if she's finished listening. "The Sword proves a Knight can fight," she says at last. "The Rose proves one can be trusted not to, when it isn't the answer." She sets a rose-worked circlet in your hands, plain silver beneath the ornament. "Wear it. You've made the case yourself, same as every one of us had to."
GIVER the Rose Knight at High Clerist's Tower
REQUIRE rose_eligible
VISIT plains_of_dust Walk the long road to the Plains of Dust and back a second time
SLAY ogre 1 Meet an ogre in single combat and come home standing
REWARD_STEEL 100
REWARD_XP 250
REWARD_KNIGHT_ROSE
END

QUEST ore_for_the_forge
NAME Ore for the Forge
OFFER "Whoever you're looking for, it isn't me," the journeyman says, setting down his hammer long enough to size you up. "I mind the forge when Flint's off wherever Flint's off to. This blade would've been done a week ago if Pax Tharkas' ore hadn't slowed to a trickle -- everyone up there's too busy arguing over who owns the mountain to dig anything out of it. You headed that way at all? Even a stray chunk would keep this forge lit a while longer."
ACCEPT You know the sort of place he means -- a mine nobody's quite in charge of anymore, which usually means nobody's minding what leaves it, either.
PROGRESS The journeyman glances at the cold half of the forge. "Still waiting on that ore, unless you're telling me otherwise."
COMPLETE The journeyman turns the ore over in his hands, weighing it like it's worth more than it looks. "That'll do it." He sets it by the anvil and picks his hammer back up. "Flint'll wonder how I finished this without him. I won't be the one to tell him."
GIVER the journeyman at Flint's Smithy
DELIVER raw_tharkadan_ore 1 Bring raw ore from the Tharkadan Mine at Pax Tharkas
REWARD_STEEL 35
REWARD_XP 80
END

QUEST staff_of_striking_curing
NAME A Priest's Rod
OFFER The scavenger turns the rod over once more before offering it, considering you the way an appraiser considers a coin. "I said priest's work. I meant it needs a priest -- somebody the gods still listen to." Their eyes drift past you, toward the black mouth of the well shaft. "Something's nesting down there now. Wasn't, when I first climbed in. I'd want it gone before I trusted anyone with what I pulled out of this place."
ACCEPT You understand the trade being offered, unstated as it is: clear out whatever's taken up residence in the well shaft, and the rod is yours to make sense of.
PROGRESS The scavenger is still crouched at the well shaft's rim, satchel between their knees. "Still down there, whatever it is. I can hear it some nights."
COMPLETE The scavenger listens to what you found below without much surprise -- "Should've guessed, this deep under the Cataclysm's mess" -- and hands over the rod without further ceremony. "It's yours to puzzle out now. I only ever felt it go cold in my hands."
GIVER the Ruin-Scavenger at Xak Tsaroth
REQUIRE cleric
SLAY skeleton 2 Clear whatever has nested in the well shaft below Xak Tsaroth
REWARD_STEEL 40
REWARD_XP 100
REWARD_STAFF_OF_STRIKING_CURING
END

QUEST frostreaver_salvage
NAME A Blade None of Us Can Lift
OFFER "You want to see something useless?" The young knight leads you to a corner where a heavy axe of blue-white ice leans against the stone, dark stains still crusted along the haft. "Pulled it off an Ice Folk raider we found frozen by the east wall -- thanoi got him before we did, tusks through the throat, whatever quarrel he died in the middle of. None of us can so much as lift the thing off the ground. If the walrus-men are still testing our gate on his account, thin their numbers and it's yours. Better used than melting in a corner."
ACCEPT You understand the trade plainly enough: two thanoi dead, and a dead man's axe you might actually be strong enough to swing.
PROGRESS "Still leaning right where I left it," the young knight says, glancing toward the corner. "The walrus-men haven't thinned themselves yet, far as I've heard."
COMPLETE "Two, you said?" He looks almost disappointed not to have counted them himself. "That's more thanoi than my whole watch has managed since the ice took Feal-thas." He nods toward the corner. "Take it, then. Wield it better than the man we found it on ever got the chance to."
GIVER the Young Knight at Ice Wall Castle
REQUIRE str_13
SLAY thanoi 2 Thin the thanoi still testing Ice Wall's gate
REWARD_STEEL 50
REWARD_XP 120
REWARD_FROSTREAVER
END
