POI D "An Ice-Bound Dragon" A silver dragon, easily forty feet long, is sealed within a wall of clear ice, wings spread as if still mid-flight. Its face holds no rage, only a vast, patient sorrow. Stranger still are the scraps of harness at its shoulders -- this dragon carried a rider, once, and whoever they were, no one here has found a body to match.
POI K "A Young Knight" Barely older than a squire, he keeps watch over the ruined hall with a spear that shakes only slightly in the cold. Whatever he saw fighting through this place to reach it, he hasn't slept well since.
POI F "Where Feal-thas Fell" Scorch marks and frost-shattered stone mark where the dark elf who called himself Dragonlord made his last stand, guarding an orb he'd sworn to keep from anyone's hands but his own. He kept that oath about as well as anyone keeps an oath to a dragon queen.
TALK K "Another one come looking for the orb? You're late. It's already gone -- south, with the ones who took it." He shifts his grip on the spear, eyes flicking to the dark beyond the torchlight. "The walrus-men don't know that yet. Mind you don't linger long enough to explain it to them."
TALK_AGAIN K "Still here, and still no walrus-men through that door. I'll count that as a good day."
TALK_AFTER K The young knight's watchfulness eases into something closer to disbelief, remembering. "The company that found the orb before it went south -- I was here for that. A knight in borrowed cold-weather gear stood at that gate like he'd done it a hundred times, though I'd wager it was closer to his first real siege than his hundredth. A dwarf wouldn't let go of a heap of our own gear the whole fight, war-axe out, daring the dark to try him for it. A kender catalogued frost patterns on these walls while the rest of us were still shaking." His voice drops. "And there was a woman with them who didn't freeze when the wolf-lord's magic held every other blade still as stone. Cut him down herself while trained knights stood there like statues. I've thought about that more than I'd like to admit, this far from anywhere warm." He shakes his head slowly. "Long gone south by now, orb and all. Whatever they were carrying it toward, I hope it was worth what it cost getting out of here."
SAY_IF K fighter He looks you over, spear easing a fraction. "You've the look of someone who's held a line before. Good. We lost two of ours clearing this hall -- Thanoi, winter wolves, worse. I'd rather not lose a third to bad company in the dark."
TOPIC K "The Walrus-Men" "Thanoi, the Ice Barbarians call them -- and barbarian's the wrong word for people who've survived out here since before this castle had a roof. The Thanoi are the real terror of this coast: tusked, vicious, and they don't tire of a fight the way a man does." He shudders. "We were glad of every wall still standing between us and them."
TOPIC K "The Voyage South" "Our ship makes for Sancrist next, with the orb and whatever's left of us aboard." He nods vaguely southeast. "The captain says we'll pass within sight of Southern Ergoth on the way -- elves living there now, he tells me, driven off their own lands by the dragonarmies. I can't say I'll be sorry to see any coastline that isn't this one."
SUBJECT K dragon,silver Sealed in that ice since before any of us got here, far as anyone can tell. No rage on its face, just something sadder than that. Scraps of harness at its shoulders say somebody rode it once. Nobody's found who.
SUBJECT K feal-thas,dragonlord Called himself Dragonlord, guarded that orb like it was worth dying for. Turned out he was right about the dying part. I try not to think too hard about the rest of what he called himself.
