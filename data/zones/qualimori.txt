POI S "The Speaker's House" A long timber hall raised beneath the spruce and broadleaf canopy, tents ringed close around it in every direction. This is as near a seat of government as the Qualinesti have left -- Speaker Solostaran's own son Porthios holds audience here now, in a house built from whatever the refugees could carry and cut.
POI G "The Guest Quarters" A cluster of hastily-raised tents set aside for travelers passing through, close enough to the Speaker's House to be watched, far enough to be politely ignored. Whoever slept here last broke camp in a hurry -- a torch still smolders in its sconce, barely an hour cold.
