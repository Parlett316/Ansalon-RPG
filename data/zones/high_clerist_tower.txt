POI C "The Chapel of the High Clerist" A small chapel just inside the walls, kept unlocked though its altar has stood bare since the Cataclysm. It's easy to picture a young man in borrowed white robes kneeling here through a long winter night, waiting for dawn and a blessing he wasn't sure would come.
POI D "The Sealed Inner Doors" Twin steel doors, barred from within, mark the one way into the Tower proper -- and no one alive holds the office that would let them open. The knights who garrison this fortress didn't build it. They only get to defend what they can't enter.
POI B "A Stair to the Battlements" Stone steps climb into the dark toward the parapet above, where the wind never quite stops and the pass below is visible for miles in every direction that matters. No one goes up without a reason. Most nights, at least one knight finds one anyway.
POI K "A Garrison Knight" He stands his post with a Solamnic formality that hasn't cracked yet, though the hollows under his eyes suggest it's costing him something to hold.
POI Y "The Muster Yard" A wide stretch of open ground where the garrison forms up at dawn, and where the Order still gathers whenever it has cause to name a new Knight in fact as well as blood. Whether that cause comes often or rarely seems to depend entirely on which years you ask about.
TALK K "Stand where I can see you, and state your business." He doesn't lower his spear, exactly, but he doesn't raise it either. "Supplies from Vingaard stopped coming weeks ago. We've gotten particular about visitors since."
TALK_AGAIN K "Still standing, are we both. That's no small thing, up here, these days."
SAY_IF K fighter The knight's formal posture eases, just slightly -- soldier recognizing soldier. "Then you'll understand why I'd rather trust a blade's read of this siege than a stranger's optimism. Whatever comes through that gate, I'm glad it won't only be knights meeting it."
TOPIC K "The Knights' Trial" The knight's voice drops, careful. "You'll have heard by now -- Lord Derek Crownguard called one of ours a coward before the whole assembly at Sancrist, and very nearly made it stick. Lord Gunthar found a door the Measure left open and walked him through it before Derek could nail it shut." He shakes his head. "Split the Order clean in half, that trial did. Half of us are still deciding which half we're standing in."
TOPIC K "Holding the Pass" The knight doesn't look away from the northern approach. "Palanthas stands or falls on whether this Tower does. That's not boast, it's arithmetic -- there's no other way through these mountains a dragonarmy could use. We hold it as long as we're able, and we don't discuss out loud what happens to Vingaard's people if we're not able long enough."
