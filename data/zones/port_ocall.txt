POI D "The Dockmaster" A broad-shouldered man in a heavy oilskin logs each boat's catch against a slate board, barely glancing up as the ferry ties off behind you.
POI Q "The Coast Road" A rutted track climbs away from the last house toward the foothills, worn smooth by generations of cart traffic. A weathered post marks the way: Palanthas, north.
TALK D "Made it across in one piece, then." He taps the slate without looking up. "Ferry runs back to Crossing whenever she's loaded -- no charge for the trip down, if that's where you're headed next."
TALK_AGAIN D "Tide's fine for the crossing, if you're heading back," he says, still logging.
BOAT D crossing 3
POI S "The Netmender's Forge" A forge that used to mend nothing but hooks and gaffs now turns out plain blades and boiled-leather jacks for anyone walking the Tower road north. The smith still smells more of fish than of coal.
SHOP S armory
POI B "A Beachcomber's Stall" A driftwood table heaped with what the strait washes up -- most of it worthless, but the old woman minding it swears a few pieces still carry a working charm, salvaged off ships that never made land.
TALK B "Half of what washes up here's junk, and I sell it as junk, mostly." She doesn't look up from sorting a tangle of cord and sea-glass. "But the sea doesn't give everything back for nothing, dear. Some of it still remembers who it belonged to."
TALK_AGAIN B "Tide's still bringing things in," she says, turning a shell over in her fingers before setting it aside. "Always does, eventually."
SHOP B magic
QUEST B what_the_tide_kept
POI W "The Storm-Wrack" A tangle of splintered spars and waterlogged rope, thrown up past the tideline by the last real storm -- driftwood, mostly, the same as everything else along this stretch, except for the one thing glinting where the wood's pulled apart.
TALK W You dig through the wrack and come away with a tarnished locket, still fastened shut, its chain snapped clean through. Nobody else seems to have noticed it.
TALK_AGAIN W The rest of the wrack is just driftwood now -- whatever else washed up here, it isn't giving up anything more.
GRANTS_ITEM W drowned_sailors_locket A Drowned Sailor's Locket
