POI J "Dargaard Keep, Distant" Higher up the mountain, visible only as a broken silhouette against the sky, stands the keep itself -- fire-blackened, crumbling, older than the Cataclysm that scarred it. No path from this clearing climbs any closer, and nothing that knows better goes looking for one.
POI G "The Forest Clearing" A small clearing hemmed in by ancient pines, their limbs so thick and tangled overhead that little daylight reaches the ground. The dirt here has been disturbed recently -- scuffed, and darker in one patch than the rest -- though nothing living stays around to explain why.
POI C "The Cave Mouth" A cave mouth opens in the cliff face at the clearing's edge, carved rather than natural, and utterly dark past the first few feet. Nothing bars the way in. Nothing about it invites the attempt, either.
