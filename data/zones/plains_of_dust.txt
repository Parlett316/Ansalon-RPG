POI Y "A Felt Tent" A round tent of stretched hide and felt, staked low against the wind. Smoke curls from a vent at its peak, and a horse stands hobbled nearby, unbothered by your approach.
POI F "The Council Fire" A wide ring of stones holds a bed of ash and embers, banked but never quite let go out. Marks scratched into the surrounding earth look deliberate, though their meaning isn't yours to read.
POI R "A Rider" A Plainswoman sits easy on a shaggy horse at the camp's edge, watching the horizon more than she watches you. She has the look of someone who could outrun trouble or ride straight through it, whichever the day calls for.
TALK R Strangers on foot don't usually make it this far without an escort or a good reason. You've got neither that I can see, so I'll ask plainly: what brings you to our fires?
TALK_AGAIN R Back again. You still haven't given me a good reason, but you also haven't caused trouble, so I suppose that's reason enough to let you sit a while.
