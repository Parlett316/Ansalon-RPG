POI T "A Marble Tower" One of Haven's six slender marble towers, strung along the city's low decorative wall like beads on a necklace. Up close the gold trim looks thinner than the stories claim, flaking in places no one's bothered to touch up.
POI M "The Seeker Temple" White marble steps climb to a colonnaded entrance where the Theocracy of Seekers holds its endless search for the true gods. Robed acolytes move in and out without ever quite meeting a stranger's eye.
POI G "A Seeker Guard" A guard stands watch near the temple steps, more decorative than the wall behind him if it weren't for the very real blade at his hip. He straightens when you get too close, then relaxes half a beat too late.
POI K "Market Stalls" A row of stalls sells dried fish, woven cloth, and small clay idols of gods no one's ever proven exist. The vendors haggle with the easy patience of people who've been doing this since before the Theocracy had a name for itself.
TALK G Move along, friend -- nothing to see at the temple today. The Highseeker's hearing petitioners, and I've got a line of them and a headache to keep in order. If you're not here about the gods, best you find somewhere else to stand.
TALK_AGAIN G Still here, still not here about the gods, I'd wager. Fine by me -- you're one less petitioner in that line.
TALK_AFTER G The guard's manner eases, just a little, once he's sure you're not one of them come back. "They came through, a while back -- eight of them, near enough, though I only had eyes on a few at a time." He counts off without much order. "A half-elf who argued with a farmer about the north road like his life hung on winning it. A dwarf who watched the Highseeker's procession like he'd seen the trick before and wasn't impressed twice. A big fellow near-carrying a thin one who couldn't stop coughing." A pause, and something almost fond creeps into his voice despite himself. "And a kender. Small one. Spent the better part of an hour asking me fool questions about gods nobody's proven exist, like he actually wanted the answers." He shakes his head. "Haven't had a headache like that since. Almost miss it."
TALK_BEFORE G Quiet enough today, if that's what you're asking. Roads have been busier than usual lately, though -- more strangers passing through than the Theocracy's used to, and not the pilgrim kind. Word's it's only going to get worse before it gets better. Keep half an eye open if you're headed east.
SAY_IF G cleric The guard's wariness sharpens into something closer to unease when his eyes settle on you. "A cleric. Real one, or another of the ones who talk to gods nobody's ever proven exist?" He doesn't wait for an answer. "No offense meant. It's just -- some days I wonder if anyone in that temple actually believes what they're searching for, or if the searching's the whole point by now."
TOPIC G "The Highseeker's Line" He jerks a thumb back toward the temple steps without much enthusiasm. "Petitioners, same as every day. Farmers wanting rain blessed, merchants wanting luck blessed, at least one fellow last week wanting his mother-in-law blessed somewhere unpleasant. The Highseeker hears every one of them like it's the first time he's heard it. I don't know if that's devotion or just very good acting. Either way, it's a long line and a longer headache."
SUBJECT G temple,seekers,highseeker Highseeker hears every petitioner like it's the first time he's heard it, day after day. I used to think that was devotion. Some days I still do. Other days it just looks like a very patient kind of tired.
SUBJECT G market,stalls,idols Vendors two streets over will sell you a clay god for a copper piece, no questions asked about which one you're buying. Good business. I try not to think too hard about the theology.
SUBJECT G war,dragons,army,armies He lowers his voice, like even a temple guard doesn't like saying it too loud. "Word from every direction says there's a war building, dragons and all, though nobody official's said so outright. All I know for certain is the roads are busier than they used to be, and the new faces aren't pilgrims. Draw your own conclusions. I've mostly stopped needing to draw mine."
SUBJECT_UNKNOWN G The guard shakes his head. "Not my department, that one. Try the temple. Bring patience -- the line's long."
