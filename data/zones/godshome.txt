POI T "The Narrow Cleft" A narrow tunnel-mouth splits the rock here, barely wide enough to crawl through -- the only way in or out of this place that anyone has ever found. Mud and old scrapes along the stone mark where broader shoulders than yours have squeezed past before.
POI C "The Circle of Standing Stones" A ring of huge, weathered boulders stands at the center of the bowl, matched so closely that nothing can be seen between them from outside. Within, by all report, lies a pool of polished black rock that holds the night sky even at noon -- a threshold, some say, rather than a place. No living thing is known to have walked out onto it twice.
POI F "Where Flint Fell" Bare gray stone stretches flat and lifeless here, the whole bowl around it just as barren -- but something about this particular patch of ground makes visitors go quiet without quite knowing why.
