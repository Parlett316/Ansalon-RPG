POI T "The Temple of the Dark Queen" A black spire leans over the compound at an angle no mason ever intended, its upper reaches lost in shadow the flying citadels left behind even now that the citadels themselves are gone. Something enormous happened here, recently enough that the dust hasn't finished settling. No one who was inside when it happened seems willing to describe it twice.
POI D "The Dungeons" A stairway spirals down into cells and corridors cut from the living rock beneath the Temple. Broken locks, scorched stone, and old blood tell most of the story without anyone having to say a word of it. Whatever fought its way free of down there, it wasn't cheap.
POI G "A Deserting Guard" A dragonarmy soldier stands half in the shadow of a collapsed tent, stripping the insignia from his own armor with shaking hands. He doesn't look like he's planning on being here much longer, and he doesn't look like anyone's inclined to stop him.
TALK G The soldier doesn't look up from the buckle he's fighting with. "Wasting your breath asking me for orders. Half the officers who'd give them are dead, and the other half are too busy running to notice anybody's still watching the gate." He finally gets the buckle loose and lets the breastplate fall. "Six Highlords marched in here ranked by seniority, like that was ever going to matter. I watched three of them scatter before the fires even properly caught. Whatever's coming next, it isn't going to have my name on the roster."
TALK_AGAIN G "Still here?" He eyes you like that's a personal failing. "I'd have been gone by now if I had anywhere better to run to. That's the whole problem with an army this size collapsing all at once -- turns out most of us never had a plan that didn't start with somebody else giving the orders."
TOPIC G "A Broken Chain of Command" "You want to know how it started coming apart? Dragons, is how. Highlords each keep their own, and not one of them trusts another's not eyeing his territory." He shakes his head. "Heard a green killed one of the Dark Queen's own reds over a kill, if you can believe that, and got caged for it under the city like that solved anything. That's the kind of army this was, the last few days. Too many masters, and every one of them certain the others were the problem."
POI S "The Temple Square" What's left of the Temple's central yard, scorched black and littered with broken tent-poles and cast-off dragonarmor no one came back to claim. It's quiet now in the particular way a battlefield goes quiet afterward -- not peace, just the absence of anyone left to keep fighting. Overhead, the sky is just sky again, for the first time in longer than anyone here can remember.
