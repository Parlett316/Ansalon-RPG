POI H "The Wedding Hill" A gentle, grassy rise at the valley's heart, worn bare at its crown where a great many feet once stood in a torchlit circle. Autumn leaves still catch in the grass here longer than anywhere else in the vale, as though the ground itself hasn't finished remembering the day.
POI C "The Refugee Shelters" Rough-built lean-tos and turf huts crowd this stretch of ground, thrown up fast against the mountain winter by people who lost everything but each other at Pax Tharkas. Smoke curls from a dozen cookfires; somewhere, a child is laughing, which after everything still sounds like the strangest sound in the world.
POI S "The Council Circle" A ring of stumps and flat stones marks where the valley's first war-council sat -- humans, dwarves, and the odd Solamnic voice, arguing out loud what to do about a dragon orb, a broken order of knights, and a mountain kingdom that hasn't opened its gates to an outsider in living memory.
