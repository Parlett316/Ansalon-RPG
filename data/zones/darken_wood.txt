POI R "Old Ruins" Broken stonework half-swallowed by moss and root, too old for any name to survive on it. The ground nearby is oddly bare, as if something is still keeping the forest back.
POI F "A Faded Trail" The path forward is clear enough, but looking back, you can't quite recall which way you came from. The trees seem to have rearranged themselves while your back was turned.
POI U "A White Shape Among the Trees" A unicorn watches you from the shadows between two great trunks, still as carved stone. It doesn't flee, and it doesn't approach -- only watches, with an attention that feels like it's weighing something about you.
TALK U The unicorn's voice is not a voice at all, more a certainty that arrives fully formed in your thoughts. "Not all who enter here mean harm. Fewer still leave with all they came in with. Walk carefully, and remember what you can."
TALK_AGAIN U The certainty returns, quieter this time, almost familiar. "You again. The wood remembers you a little now, too. That is not nothing."
TALK_AFTER U The certainty that follows is slower this time, as though turning over something already decided. "Eight of your kind passed beneath these trees, not long ago, each carrying a different weight into my judgment. A half-elf carried a decision already made and could not stop re-tallying it. A golden-eyed one breathed shallow the whole way, reading warning into every shadow as though the trees spoke a language only he understood. A big one kept a hand loose on his sword, hungrier for a rabbit to shoot than a ghost to meet. An old dwarf's ghost stories lost their conviction the deeper the path closed in around him. A healer never once let go of what she carried, as though it might need to answer for something. A plainsman read the ground and trusted it less with every step, which was wise of him. One walked at the front the entire way, deciding for the rest whether each turn was safe before it was his to decide. And a small one sat in a patch of sunlight sketching my wood as though it already belonged to him, and was not entirely wrong to feel that way." The certainty settles, quieter than before. "I judged each of them. I let each of them pass. I have not yet decided whether that says more about them, or about what I am willing to forgive."
SAY_IF U good The certainty that arrives in your thoughts this time carries something almost like warmth. "Your weight is a light one. Not all who enter Darken Wood come to take -- some, rarer than you'd think, come only to give, or to give more than they take. I have not forgotten how rare that is."
SAY_IF U evil The certainty turns colder, and it lingers a moment longer than before, as if measuring you twice. "I have judged your kind before, and let some pass regardless -- not all who enter here mean harm to the wood itself, whatever harm they mean elsewhere. Do not mistake my patience for blindness. I am not finished weighing you yet."
TOPIC U "Guardian of the Wood" The thought that answers is unhurried, ancient. "I am not the wood's only guardian, but I am the one who receives those who cross into it uninvited. I judge what I am shown -- not by wealth, not by strength, but by what a life has given more than it has taken. Some who enter here are turned back gently. Some are not turned back at all. You may judge for yourself which you have been."
SUBJECT U ruins,stones The stones you passed were old before this wood learned your kind's name for it. Something stood there once. The wood remembers only that it does not stand there now, and asks no more of the ground than that.
SUBJECT U trail,path,lost The path that confused you was never lost. You were. The wood does not rearrange itself for cruelty's sake -- only to see who keeps walking anyway, and who turns back to blame the trees.
SUBJECT U guardian,judge,judgment The certainty that answers carries no doubt at all. "I judge everyone who crosses into this wood uninvited, and I have never once judged by strength or wealth or the cut of a traveler's cloth. Only by what a life has given more than it has taken. Most who ask this question already suspect the answer they will not like."
SUBJECT_UNKNOWN U The certainty in your thoughts goes still, unanswering. Whatever you asked, the wood has not yet decided it is yours to know.
QUEST U what_the_stones_remember
