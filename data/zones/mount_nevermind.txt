POI G "The Gnomeflinger" A device of pulleys, coiled springs, and one deeply unhappy-looking counterweight sits half-assembled here, surrounded by gnomes arguing in Common spoken at three times a comfortable listening speed. Nobody stops to explain what it's for. Nobody looks entirely sure, themselves.
POI P "The Philosopher's Guild Hall" A cavernous chamber lined floor to ceiling with slate boards, most covered in half-erased equations and at least one long-running argument conducted entirely in chalk. A small brass plaque names it after a question nobody present remembers the answer to anymore.
POI W "A Window Shaft" A steep shaft climbs toward daylight far overhead, fitted with mirrors and lenses on complicated pivoting arms. Sunlight arrives at the bottom in a single, wavering, thoroughly over-engineered beam, exactly bright enough to read by, on a good day.
POI R "Research" A workbench crowded with disassembled clockwork and at least one item that is unmistakably, if inexplicably, on fire in a controlled and apparently intentional way. Nobody here seems alarmed by this.
POI C "The Examination Room" A stone chamber built for taking things apart to see how they work, its walls lined with calipers, magnifying lenses on articulated arms, and diagrams of things that were probably whole, once. Something large and round currently occupies the center table, surrounded by gnomes who have clearly been arguing about it for some time.
POI M "The Geothermal Station" Steam vents hiss steadily from a fissure in the rock here, feeding a tangle of pipes and valves that hum, judder, and occasionally shriek in a way every gnome nearby insists is completely normal.
