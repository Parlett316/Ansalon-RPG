POI G "The Guardians" A row of statues, worn past recognizing, lines the path here -- watching, or once meant to. Whatever they were carved to guard, they've long since stopped being able to stop anyone from reaching it.
POI T "Huma's Tomb" A colossal dragon shape is carved straight out of the living cliff face above the tomb's black stone entrance -- work no hand of dwarf, elf, or human ever did, and older by far than the tomb it watches over. Inside, a bier holds a shield and sword nobody here has dared to touch. The air smells faintly of crushed rose petals.
POI D "The Dragon's Throat" A narrow, wind-carved passage climbs up into the mountain's stone, opening at last into a vast chamber shaped like something's gullet. Faded murals wrap the walls in every direction -- dragons of every color, and above them, dragons of silver and gold.
