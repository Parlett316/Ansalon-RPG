POI T "The Ancestors' Temple" A round stone temple stands here, or did -- its walls have run and cooled in long grey folds, like candlewax left too near a flame. Whatever ancestor-carvings once lined it are gone entirely, melted into the slump of stone at its base.
POI H "The Chieftain's Home" Little is left standing of the Chieftain's own house but a single corner wall. A shattered clay vase lies among the rubble here, its painted pieces too scattered and too small for anyone to ever fit back together.
POI G "The Sunken Arena" A bowl-shaped depression where the village's arena once stood, its stone tiers slumped into rippled, half-melted waves. A gibbet stands in the center on blackened grass -- two scorched posts, a charred crosspiece, three chains still swinging with a slow, tuneless creak. A battered shield hangs from it, a warning scratched into the metal in crude Common and signed with a name none of you yet recognize: Verminaard.
POI W "The Grieving Wall" A long stretch of the village's outer wall, less ruined than the rest, curves away toward the eastern road. Villagers once brought their dead here to be mourned before the Ancestors' Temple claimed them. Nothing about that custom survived whatever burned this place.
