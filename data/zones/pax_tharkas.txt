POI T "A Twin Tower" One of the fortress's two great guard towers, flanking the pass it was built to hold. The stonework predates every banner that's flown from it since.
POI M "The Tharkadan Mine Entrance" A wide iron-bound gate opens into the mountain itself, dwarven-cut and elven-mortared, older than the war currently fought over who controls what's dug from it.
POI O "An Ore Cart" A handcart of raw ore sits abandoned near the gate, half-loaded and going to rust -- whoever left it here in a hurry hasn't come back for it, and with the mine's ownership still an open question, it doesn't look like anyone's about to ask who it belongs to.
TALK O You dig through the cart and come away with a solid chunk of raw ore, heavier than it looks. Nobody stops you. Nobody's watching closely enough to.
TALK_AGAIN O The cart's mostly picked over now, whatever's left not worth the trouble of digging for twice.
GRANTS_ITEM O raw_tharkadan_ore Raw Tharkadan Ore
POI G "A Fortress Guard" A soldier stands post in the courtyard, watching the road south more than he watches you. Whoever holds Pax Tharkas this season, the pass still needs watching.
TALK G Move through if you're moving through. The road south is open today, for whatever that's worth -- I wouldn't count on it staying that way, and I wouldn't ask me who's counting.
TALK_AGAIN G Still standing, same as you. Road's still open. I'd tell you that's a good sign, but I've been wrong about that before.
SAY_IF G fighter The guard's posture doesn't relax, exactly, but it shifts -- soldier recognizing soldier. "You'd know, then, how a place like this gets held as much by who's willing to stand the watch as by how thick the walls are. I'll say no more than that. Walls have ears, and so do the men standing in front of them."
TOPIC G "A Fortress Between Masters" He looks past you, out toward the pass, rather than at you. "Pax Tharkas has outlasted every banner that's ever flown over it. Dwarves cut it, elves mortared it, and I couldn't begin to tell you how many armies have marched through since and called it theirs for a season. The mountain doesn't much care whose flag is up that week. Neither, most days, do I. I just watch the road."
SUBJECT G mine,tharkadan,ore Whoever actually owns that mine changes depending on who you ask and which week it is. Meantime the ore backs up and nobody digs, and towns downroad go without steel while the argument sorts itself out. I just watch the road. Not my argument to settle.
SUBJECT G towers,twin Two towers, same age as the pass itself, flanking it same as they always have regardless of whose banner's flying that season. Outlasted every army that's marched through here. I expect they'll outlast this one too.
SUBJECT_UNKNOWN G He shrugs, eyes still on the road south. "Wouldn't know. Ask somebody who's not just watching."
