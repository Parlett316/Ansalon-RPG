POI I "Inn of the Last Home" A broad stair spirals up the vallenwood trunk to a heavy door standing open. Firelight and the smell of Otik's spiced potatoes spill out into the square.
PORTAL I solace_inn
POI T "Vallenwood Tree" One of the great trees that hold Solace aloft. Rope bridges creak between its branches, high overhead.
POI B "Notice Board" A weathered board nailed to a trunk, thick with layers of old notices. The newest ones speak of armies on the move in the east.
TALK B You study the crowded board. Most of the notices are old news by now -- a lost dog, a lean-to for hire -- but one hand-lettered scrap looks fresher than the rest.
TALK_AGAIN B The board hasn't changed since you last read it, except for whatever's freshly pinned up.
QUEST B road_wolves
POI G "General Store" A cluttered storefront built into a hollowed vallenwood limb, shelves crowded with rope, oil, dried rations, and traveling gear.
SHOP G
POI S "Flint's Smithy" A stocky dwarf hunches over the forge, muttering at a half-finished blade. He doesn't look up, but he's clearly listening.
TALK S "Whoever you're looking for, it isn't me," the journeyman says, setting down his hammer just long enough to size you up. "I mind the forge when Flint's off wherever Flint's off to. Which is most of the time, these days." He nods at the half-finished blade. "Would've been done a week ago if Pax Tharkas' ore hadn't slowed to a trickle -- everyone up there's too busy arguing over who owns the mountain to dig anything out of it."
TALK_AGAIN S The forge is still going, the blade still waiting on its ore. "Same as before," the journeyman says, without looking up this time.
SUBJECT S flint,dwarf,forge Learned everything I know about a hammer from him, and he never once let me forget how much further I've got to go. Best smith in Solace, when he's here. Which these days is a question with more silence in it than answer.
SUBJECT S ore,steel,blade Can't finish good steel without good ore, and the ore's not coming down from Pax Tharkas the way it used to. Half-finished blade's been sitting on that anvil a week longer than it should. I'd fix it myself if fixing it were as simple as wanting to.
SUBJECT_UNKNOWN S The journeyman shrugs without looking up from the anvil. "Ask Flint that, when he turns up. I just mind the forge."
QUEST S ore_for_the_forge
SHOP S armory
SHOP_LOCKED S ore_for_the_forge
POI K "Bren Alder" A broad-shouldered young man leans against a vallenwood trunk, running a whetstone along a well-worn shortsword. He watches every loaded pack and travel-stained cloak that passes with open envy.
TALK K "You're headed out from Solace, aren't you? I can tell by the pack." He sheathes the blade and straightens up. "Patched roofs and hauled lumber in this town my whole life. Never been further than Haven. If you'll have me, I'd rather swing this sword at something worth fighting than spend another season up a vallenwood."
TALK_AGAIN K Bren gives you a ready nod, whetstone still in hand.
RECRUIT K bren_alder
END
