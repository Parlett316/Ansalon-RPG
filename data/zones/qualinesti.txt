POI T "A Silver Tower" One of four slender towers marking the corners of the city, lined in silver that catches the light without ever quite glinting harshly. A chain of matching silver arches strings between it and its neighbors.
POI G "The Golden Tower" A tall spire of burnished gold dominates the city's center, throwing off a slow, sparkling shimmer even in still air. It looks like it should be moving. It isn't.
POI H "The Hall of the Sky" An open square open to the stars rather than any roof -- Qualinost's central gathering place, where the Speaker's people bring both celebrations and hard news.
POI E "An Elven Sentinel" She doesn't block your path, exactly, but she doesn't quite let you forget she's there either -- a hand resting near her blade, her eyes tracking you with the polite, unhurried wariness of someone who has watched a great many outsiders come and go.
TALK E You've found your way further into Qualinesti than most are shown. That's not an accusation -- only an observation. Speak your business plainly, and you'll find my people fair. Wander with none, and you may find us less so.
TALK_AGAIN E You again. My eyes rest a little easier on a face I've already measured once. Don't take that for trust -- only for a shorter list of questions.
TALK_AFTER E Her wariness softens, just slightly, into something more like memory. "You are not the first outsiders to walk further into Qualinesti than most are shown. Eight came before you, some seasons past -- one of them born to these very halls, though it showed on him only in flashes." She considers a moment before continuing. "A mage who watched our ceremony like a man amused by his own cleverness. A dwarf who kept his opinion of our timber close to his chest, which for a dwarf is its own kind of courtesy. Two more who stood at the crowd's edge like open sky suited them better than gold towers." The faintest, dry note enters her voice. "And a kender. I walked behind that one myself, with a second sentry, the better part of a day -- and came away no wiser about how he'd slipped past the first two. He seemed to enjoy the attention more than most enjoy solitude." A pause. "They are gone from here now. Whatever brought them, I hope they found it."
SUBJECT E qualinesti,elves,people My people have called this forest home longer than most nations bother keeping count of their own years. We are patient because we have had every reason to become so. That patience is not the same as an open door.
SUBJECT E speaker,hall The Hall of the Sky is where the Speaker's people bring what matters -- celebration, grief, hard news, all under the same open stars. No walls to muffle it. I've always thought that was deliberate.
SUBJECT_UNKNOWN E Her eyes don't leave you. "That is not a question I am inclined to answer a stranger. Ask something plainer."
